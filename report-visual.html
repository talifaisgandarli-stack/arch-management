Only two-thirds of the day is the actual job.

Across ~600 industry leaders, professionals spend 65% of time on optimal work. The other 35% — roughly 14 hours every week — is searching for information, resolving conflicts, and redoing work. This is the largest recoverable line item in the industry, and it's simply accepted as "the work."

SHEET A-02
The RFI Machine · questions that cost money
Navigant · 1,362 projects
02

Every project runs a $860,000 question queue.

The landmark Navigant study of 1,362 projects put a price on the Request-For-Information: about $1,080 to review and respond to each one, ~796 per project, ~$860K per project — and more than one in five never gets an official answer. The delay lives in the "double hop" to specialist consultants, which no email thread tracks.

SHEET A-03
Rework · who actually causes it
CII · 9-project study
03

Rework is a design problem wearing a construction costume.

Direct field rework runs ~5% of total project cost (2–20%). But when the Construction Industry Institute traced the deviations behind it, design errors, omissions and changes drove 79% of the cost — not the trades. And firms under-report their own rework by ~300%, so most don't even know the number.

SHEET A-07
The Build Order · one OS, not fourteen tools
Strategy
07

Sequence the wedge into a monopoly.

Don't build "Asana for architects." Build the system of record for decisions, versions and status — then monetize the quantified leaks. Each layer references the one beneath it; the accumulated record is the moat.

1

Decision & version ledger

Bind every approval, change and RFI answer to the exact artifact version, across email / chat / meeting / site. The substrate everything else references — and the firm's portable institutional memory.

2

Change-order + FF&E capture

Turn informal requests into billable change orders; put spec ↔ budget ↔ PO ↔ delivery on one live record. Self-funding ROI in month one

3

RFI engine + client approval layer

An RFI/submittal state machine with consultant SLAs, and a versioned client-approval surface that spreads virally from firm to client.

4

Vendor-data & estimation network

Real lead-time outcomes per vendor and estimate-from-actuals — the compounding data moats no competitor can copy.

The result is not fourteen tools. It is one operating system whose defensibility is the accumulated, bound, queryable record of how a firm designs, decides, coordinates and buys. That record is the monopoly.
