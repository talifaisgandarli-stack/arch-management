The Problem02 / 14
Designers design 30% of the time. The rest is admin.

Great taste, buried under busywork.

High-end residential designers lose their days to the unglamorous middle: rebuilding moodboards by hand, chasing client sign-off over email, and re-keying specs — while the tools meant to help are either ugly, manual, or distrusted.

Lost approvals

"I never approved that."

Client sign-off lives in email and text. When it's disputed, the designer eats the rework — and the relationship.

Manual everything

Hours per moodboard

Inspiration is collected on Pinterest, then rebuilt by hand in Canva/PowerPoint. Specs are copy-pasted into spreadsheets that break.

Tools they resent

Ugly or untrusted

Incumbents feel like a "dinosaur," or are marketing machines in court over billing. Designers try them and quietly quit.
